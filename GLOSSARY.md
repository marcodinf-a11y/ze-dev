# Studio simulation

A historical game development studio economy simulation, following experimental beginnings and the growth of a studio.

## Language

**Campaign**:
A game mode with an ending at which the creative legacy across the player's studio runs is assessed. Success can come from beloved games, technical innovation, or sustained reputation without market dominance.

**Sandbox**:
An open game mode in which players choose their own goals.
_Avoid_: Open campaign

**Creative legacy**:
The lasting contribution of a studio's games and expertise, reflected in beloved games, technical innovation, or sustained reputation.

**Project**:
A studio undertaking to produce an experimental demonstration or a commercial game, with a concept and committed resources.

**Project review**:
A decision point prompted by a planned milestone, discovery, or threat, at which the player reassesses a project's direction and commitments.

**Commission**:
A project funded by a client in exchange for delivering an agreed outcome.
